Gemstone Lore & Lore

The Complete 12-Month Birthstone & Natural Gemstone Meaning Guide

✍️ SilverPetals Gemological Team • ⏱️ 6 Min Read • Updated October 2026
Each month is celebrated by unique natural crystals formed over millions of years deep in Earth's crust.

Birthstones have held spiritual, emotional, and astrological significance across civilizations for thousands of years. Whether you are selecting a deeply personal birthday gift or curating a meaningful keepsake for yourself, this guide details the history, colors, and Mohs hardness of each official month.

The Official 12-Month Calendar

January: Garnet

Celebrated for its rich pomegranate-red embers, Garnet is an ancient talisman of safety, energy, and grounding. With a Mohs hardness of 7.0–7.5, it is exceptionally durable for everyday rings.

February: Amethyst

The regal purple variety of quartz, Amethyst has symbolized serenity, mental clarity, and peace since ancient Greece. Its violet hues pair majestically with solid 925 sterling silver settings.

March: Aquamarine

Reflecting the tranquil shades of sea water, Aquamarine is a beryl crystal that represents calm, truth, and protection for travelers across oceans and life journeys.

April: Diamond & Herkimer Quartz

The hardest natural substance on earth (10 on Mohs scale), representing unbreakable love and eternal light. For bohemian jewelry lovers, double-terminated natural Herkimer quartz crystals provide identical organic clarity.

May: Emerald

The gemstone of springtime renewal and prosperity. Its verdant green hues have adorned royal crowns from Cleopatra to the Renaissance.

June: Rainbow Moonstone & Natural Pearl

June is blessed with magical stones. Rainbow Moonstone exhibits adularescence—an ethereal blue-white optical flash resembling moonlight floating across water, symbolizing new beginnings, intuition, and balance.

💎 Gemstone Hardness & Daily Wear Tip

Stones with Mohs hardness 7.0 or above (Quartz, Garnet, Sapphire) are resilient against daily knocks. For softer gems like Moonstone (6.0–6.5) or Opal (5.5–6.5), choose protective silver petal bezel settings rather than high-exposed prongs.

July: Ruby

The fiery "King of Gems," Ruby is red corundum symbolizing vitality, passion, and courage. Its radiant crimson brilliance makes it a timeless heirloom investment.

August: Peridot & Spinel

One of the few gemstones formed in magma deep within the Earth's mantle, Peridot glows with a vivid olive-lime sparkle, symbolizing abundance and lighthearted joy.

September: Sapphire

Deep velvety blue corundum honoring wisdom, truth, and loyalty. Beyond classic navy, sapphires also occur in breathtaking yellow, pink, and peach hues.

October: Opal & Tourmaline

Famous for its kaleidoscopic "play of color," Opal is one of nature's most captivating creations. Combined with watermelon and pink Tourmaline, October celebrates limitless creativity.

November: Citrine & Topaz

Radiating honeyed warmth and golden amber tones, Citrine is known as the "Merchant's Stone," representing happiness, warmth, and sunny optimism.

December: Turquoise & Tanzanite

From the Robin's-egg blue of natural matrix Turquoise to the mesmerizing violet-blue depths of rare Tanzanite, December stones honor protection, open communication, and positive luck.
