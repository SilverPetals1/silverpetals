Stunning Bathroom Upgrades Under $100

H
Written by SilverPetals • July 10, 2026 • 11 min read
Many homeowners believe that elevating a bathroom requires a massive budget and a complete construction teardown. However, you don't need a full renovation to make your washing space feel luxury and clean. Small, highly targeted upgrades can create a huge visual impact at a fraction of the cost. By focusing on cosmetic elements like plumbing hardware, cabinet pulls, modern lighting, and textured textiles, you can elevate the style of your space for less. In this article, we outline 15 smart, budget-friendly bathroom upgrades that realistically cost under $100 and can be completed in a single weekend.

1. Swapping Cabinet and Drawer Hardware

Old, basic hardware drags down the look of your entire vanity cabinet. Replacing standard handles and knobs with brushed brass, matte black, or leather T-bar pulls is a highly effective weekend upgrade. You can find packs of 10 modern cabinet handles online for around $20 to $40. Unscrew the original fixtures, wipe down the drawer fronts, and screw in the new hardware. This simple swap instantly updates the vanity, giving standard cabinets a custom, designer-like quality without the expense of replacement.

2. New Showerhead for a Spa-Like Upgrade

Upgrade your daily shower experience by replacing a basic wall showerhead with a wide, high-pressure rain showerhead. Modern stainless steel or brushed brass rain showerheads cost between $30 and $60. Wrap plumbing tape around the shower arm threads and screw the new fixture into place. The wider water distribution and gentle flow mimic the soothing sensation of spa showers. Choosing a showerhead with a handheld sprayer adds extra cleaning flexibility, improving function and style in one quick step.

3. Fresh Coat of Paint on Walls or Vanity

Paint is one of the most powerful and affordable home improvement tools. A single gallon of premium interior paint costs around $40 to $60. Choose a warm, neutral color like alabaster white, soft sage green, or muted taupe. Paint the vanity cabinet to hide scratches, or refresh scuffed wall areas near the door. The clean color covers old stains, brightens the room's layout, and creates a fresh backdrop for your styling accessories, making the entire bathroom look brand new.

4. New Mirror or Mirror Frame Upgrade

Replace a standard frameless bathroom mirror with an arched or circular mirror to add architectural interest. Alternatively, build a custom wood frame around your existing flat mirror for under $30. Glue stained pine boards directly onto the mirror edges using construction adhesive. Framing the glass conceals scuffed edges and anchors the vanity layout, turning a plain mirror into a stylish, custom-made design centerpiece.

5. Plush Towels and a Matching Bath Mat Set

Old, mismatched towels look messy and clutter the bathroom's design. Refresh your bathroom textiles by investing in a matching set of organic cotton bath towels and a plush woven bath mat in coordinate colors like sand, olive, or cream. A quality towel bundle costs around $40 to $70. Fold towels neatly over rings and place the rug in front of the sink. The soft fabric textures absorb sound and bring warmth to cold tile floors, making stepping out of the shower comfortable and cozy.

6. Peel-and-Stick Tile or Wallpaper Accent

Peel-and-stick wallpaper and tile decals are excellent, affordable solutions for dated walls. A single roll of high-quality water-resistant wallpaper costs about $30 to $50. Apply a geometric or botanical print behind the vanity mirror to define a focal wall. Peel-and-stick decals are easy to install, water-resistant, and peel off cleanly. This simple upgrade adds custom texture and visual interest without the need for permanent demolition or messy glue.

7. New Faucet or Fixture Finish Swap

Replace an old, corroded sink faucet with a modern single-handle faucet in matte black or brushed brass. High-quality, budget-friendly vanity faucets cost between $50 and $80. Shut off the under-sink water valves, disconnect the water supply lines, and install the new faucet using simple hand tools. The clean finish and sleek lines of a new faucet serve as a stylish accent on vanity tops, instantly elevating the look of the sink area.

8. Woven Baskets for Stylish Storage

Woven storage baskets are excellent organizers that conceal plastic clutter and bring natural warmth to bathroom corners. Set out a rattan hamper for dirty laundry, and place smaller seagrass baskets on vanity shelves to organize extra toilet paper and soaps. A set of three woven baskets costs around $25 to $45, adding clean texture that breaks up cold tiles and keeps toiletries organized.

9. Warm-Toned Light Bulbs or a New Light Fixture

Harsh fluorescent lighting makes bathrooms feel cold and clinical. Replace old light bulbs with warm, dimmable LED bulbs (2700K or lower) for under $15 to soften the space. Alternatively, swap an outdated vanity light bar for a modern two-light brass vanity sconce, which costs about $40 to $70. Upgrading your lighting softens hard tile edges, casts a relaxing amber glow, and helps the room feel warm and inviting.

10. Removable Wall Decals or Small Art Pieces

Bare bathroom walls can feel clinical and cold. Frame two or three small sketches or botanical prints in simple oak wood frames to add color and personality. Use frames with glass fronts to protect the artwork from humidity, and hang them using damage-free picture strips. A set of matching framed prints costs around $30 to $50, turning empty plaster walls into a curated, gallery-like styling display.

11. New Shower Curtain and Liner Upgrade

A stained or wrinkled shower curtain dominates a small bathroom, making it look worn. Replace it with a thick, textured waffle-weave cotton shower curtain and a mildew-resistant liner, which costs about $25 to $45. Choose light neutral colors like oatmeal or off-white. The heavy fabric drapes beautifully, conceals the tub area, and introduces a soft, textile layer that makes the entire room feel cozy and clean.

12. Floating Shelf for Storage and Display

Mounting a single floating shelf above the vanity or toilet provides extra space for storage and display. Choose a light white oak shelf, which costs about $20 to $35. Style it with a small plant, glass cotton swab jars, and a folded hand towel. The open storage shelf keeps counters clear, lets light flow freely, and serves as a decorative accent that breaks up flat tiled walls.

13. Greenery or a Small Plant for Freshness

Bring natural freshness into a windowless bathroom with live greenery. Select humidity-loving plants like ferns, snake plants, or pothos, which cost under $15. Place a small pot on the vanity or hang a bundle of fresh eucalyptus branches from the shower arm. The warm steam releases essential oils, filling the room with a refreshing scent and adding organic color that coordinates with neutral styling.

14. Scented Candles or Diffuser for Ambiance

Engage the sense of smell to reinforce the spa experience. Place a minimalist ceramic reed diffuser or an amber glass candle on vanity surfaces. A quality reed diffuser with lavender or eucalyptus essential oils costs around $20 to $35. The gentle scent freshens the air, while the warm flicker of candlelight creates a cozy, relaxing environment that makes mechanical fixtures unnecessary.

15. Caulk and Grout Refresh for a Clean, New Look

Discolored grout lines and cracked caulking make a bathroom look old, even if it is clean. Refresh them for under $20 using a grout pen and a tube of silicone bathroom caulk. Apply new white caulk along the edges of the tub and sink, and use a grout pen to whiten stained lines between tiles. This simple, affordable detail conceals wear, prevents water damage, and makes old tiling look brand new.

Conclusion

Upgrading your bathroom doesn't require a large budget or professional construction. By focusing on simple cosmetic changes like replacing vanity knobs, mounting a wood shelf, updating your showerhead, or applying a fresh coat of paint, you can easily elevate the style of your space for under $100. Start by choosing one or two upgrades that fit your needs best, and complete them over a weekend. You will be amazed at how much visual impact these small, affordable changes can make. Happy decorating!
