30 Modern POP Ceiling Design Ideas for Every Room

H
Written by SilverPetals • July 10, 2026 • 14 min read
POP (Plaster of Paris) ceiling designs remain a highly popular modern choice for contemporary residential interior design. Made from gypsum powder that expands when mixed with water, POP allows craftspeople to sculpt custom, three-dimensional shapes directly onto your ceiling. In this roundup, we cover 30 modern POP ceiling design ideas organized by room, helping you select a design suited to each space's function and style. let your interior styling journey unfold!

Living Room POP Ceiling Designs

1. Multi-Level POP Ceiling with Cove Lighting

A multi-level POP ceiling features overlapping rectangular plaster panels that add striking visual depth. Hide dimmable LED strip lights inside the panel recesses to wash the surfaces in warm, indirect lighting, helping large living rooms feel cozy and luxurious.

2. Geometric POP Border with Recessed Spotlights

Frame your living room seating layout with a clean, geometric POP border dropped along the room's perimeter. Install flush spotlights inside the border to cast focused light cones down the walls, highlighting paintings and bookshelves beautifully.

3. Curved POP Ceiling Design

Replace sharp panel corners with smooth, curved plaster transitions to soften living room layouts. Run warm LED light strips along the curves to cast diffuse, organic highlights that coordinate beautifully with curved sofas and round area rugs.

4. Two-Tone POP Ceiling

Paint the main central ceiling panel a soft sand color and keep the surrounding raised borders white. This color contrast creates an illusion of depth, drawing the eye upward and coordinating with neutral plaster walls and wood furniture below.

5. POP Ceiling with a Statement Chandelier Cutout

Construct a recessed central circular panel and hang a sculptural brass chandelier directly in the center. The surrounding plaster borders highlight the fixture, while the chandelier casts a warm, glittering light that elevates the room with classic luxury.

6. Circular POP Ceiling with Centered Pendant Light

A central recessed circular dome breaks up the boxy corners of bedrooms, creating an elegant architectural centerpiece. Dimmable LED strip lights along the circular border project a soft, glowing highlight that helps the sleeping area feel quiet and welcoming.

7. Tray POP Ceiling with Warm LED Backlighting

Lay warm white LED strip lights inside the hidden channel along the tray border to project a soft glow upward. This indirect ambient light reduces harsh glare, making it the perfect design system for relaxing bedtime routines.

8. Minimalist POP Border with Hidden Edge Lighting

Install a thin gypsum border running along the perimeter of the room. Mount warm LED strip lights along the inner edge of the border, leaving the main central ceiling flat, keeping the room bright, open, and spacious.

9. POP Ceiling Design Over the Bed Only

Drop a single gypsum section slightly over the bed area, keeping the surrounding walkway flat. This level change visually separates the sleeping zone, helping the bed layout feel sheltered and cozy.

10. Wave-Pattern POP Ceiling

Installing flexible POP panels shaped into fluid, undulating curves brings architectural softness and movement overhead, creating a relaxing, cloud-like canopy above the bed that helps calm the mind for sleep.

Kitchen POP Ceiling Designs

11. Simple POP Border Ceiling with Recessed Lighting

Install a simple, flat POP border along the kitchen perimeter. Run recessed spotlights inside to focus bright daylight on countertops and food prep areas, keeping walkways bright and clean.

12. POP False Ceiling with Integrated Exhaust Cutouts

Construct custom POP channels to hide bulky exhaust ventilation pipes. The plaster panels frame the ventilation pipes cleanly, maintaining a seamless visual flow above stove counters.

13. Two-Tier POP Ceiling Above the Island

Drop a rectangular POP panel directly above the central kitchen island. Hang warm glass pendants from the dropped tier to focus warm light over the dining island, dividing open kitchens visually.

14. Moisture-Resistant POP Finish

Use moisture-resistant POP mixtures for humid kitchen environments. The plaster resists sagging and staining from steam, keeping kitchen ceilings looking clean, fresh, and modern for decades.

Bathroom POP Ceiling Designs

15. Compact POP Ceiling with Waterproof Recessed Lighting

Install a flat POP panel to hide bathroom ventilation pipes. Embed waterproof recessed spotlight points inside the plaster to cast clean light over vanity sinks, keeping the layout looking organized and clean.

16. Simple Flat POP Ceiling with a Single Accent Light

Paint a flat POP ceiling a soft sand color and mount a single round globe sconce in the center, reflecting soft light that makes small bathroom layouts feel cozy and spacious.

17. POP Border Design to Define a Shower Zone

Drop a narrow POP border above the walk-in shower stall to separate it visually from the vanity counter, adding clean lines that frame bathroom layouts beautifully.

Dining Room POP Ceiling Designs

18. POP Ceiling Design Centered Around a Statement Chandelier

Construct a recessed central circular POP dome to frame a statement chandelier, casting a glittering light that centers the layout of the dining table below.

19. Coffered-Style POP Ceiling

Construct square plaster box grids using detailed moldings. The repeating grid pattern adds three-dimensional depth and classic elegance, helping dining rooms look structured and premium.

20. POP Ceiling with Soft Cove Lighting

Hide dimmable LED cove lighting along the ceiling borders. The warm light washes over the POP plaster, reflecting a soft glow that creates a cozy evening dining environment.

Home Office POP Ceiling Designs

21. Minimalist POP Ceiling with Functional Lighting

Install a flat white POP ceiling with recessed spotlights to cast bright, direct light over the desk area, reducing eye strain during long hours of office work.

22. POP Ceiling with a Subtle Geometric Accent

Add a shallow rectangular drop panel along the side wall to frame open book shelving, keeping the study layout looking structured, organized, and modern.

Kids' Room POP Ceiling Designs

23. Playful POP Ceiling Design with Soft Color Accents

Paint a recessed POP tray a soft pastel mint or baby blue color, adding cheerful warmth that makes kids' bedrooms feel cozy, bright, and welcoming.

24. POP Ceiling with Star or Cloud-Inspired Cutouts

Sculpt cloud shapes or star cutouts in the POP plaster, backlit by blue or yellow LED lights to create a fun, starry night sky effect for children.

Hallway/Entryway POP Ceiling Designs

25. Linear POP Ceiling Design to Elongate a Narrow Space

Run parallel recessed plaster channels along the length of narrow hallways, directing natural daylight forward to make entrance walkways look long and spacious.

26. POP Ceiling with Recessed Spotlighting

Mount small, warm spotlight points inside a flat entryway POP ceiling, casting a warm, welcoming light that greets visitors and coordinates with entrance decor.

General & Versatile POP Ceiling Designs

27. Textured POP Ceiling for Added Depth

Apply textured plaster patterns to a flat ceiling to break up flat surfaces, casting soft shadows that catch natural daylight beautifully at different angles.

28. POP Ceiling with Mixed Matte and Gloss Finishes

Combine a matte plaster border with a glossy stretch-fabric central panel to reflect light, creating a high-contrast finish that helps low ceilings feel tall.

29. POP Ceiling Combined with Wooden Beam Accents

Mount exposed wood beams horizontally across a white POP ceiling. The warm oak timber details add structural charm and rustic warmth to contemporary spaces.

30. Symmetrical POP Panel Design for a Balanced Look

Partition the ceiling into a grid of four square POP panels, each holding a recessed spotlight. This clean, balanced arrangement helps room layouts feel calm, restful, and organized.
