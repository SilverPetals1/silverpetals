How Do Zevo Refill Cartridges Work?

Zevo Flying Insect Trap Refill Cartridges are specialized adhesive inserts engineered to snap directly into compatible Zevo plug-in bases. The system operates on a proven bio-attractant principle:

  1. Dual-Spectrum Light Attraction: The plug-in base emits a precise spectrum of blue and ultraviolet (UV) light that mimics natural visual cues, strongly drawing flying insects toward the device 24/7.
  2. Trap-and-Lock Adhesive: When insects approach the light, they enter the recessed cartridge chamber and make contact with an industrial-strength adhesive backing.
  3. Concealed Design: Unlike unsightly yellow ribbons or exposed sticky flypaper, the adhesive surface faces inward toward the wall. Dead insects remain hidden from plain view during everyday use.
  4. Clean, Touch-Free Removal: Built with a dedicated "No-Mess Tab", you can pull out the loaded cartridge and dispose of it directly into the trash without ever touching a dead insect or sticky glue.

What Types of Insects Do Zevo Refills Catch?

According to manufacturer laboratory data and extensive homeowner feedback, Zevo standard and MAX refill cartridges effectively capture common household flying pests:

  • Fruit Flies: Highly attracted to the light signature, especially when placed near fruit bowls, trash bins, or kitchen sinks.
  • Gnats & Fungus Gnats: Readily drawn from over-watered indoor plant soil to the blue/UV glow.
  • House Flies & Drain Flies: Wandering flies entering open doors or breeding in organic drain slime.
  • Non-Biting Midges & Small Moths: Attracted to interior night lights and captured silently.

Are They Good for Fruit Flies?

Yes. Fruit flies are notoriously responsive to Zevo's light spectrum. Placing a plug-in unit in an electrical outlet above your kitchen counter or next to a fruit basket consistently intercepts foraging adults before they can lay eggs. However, remember that no trap can stop fruit flies if rotting fruit or unwashed juice containers remain accessible breeding grounds.

Can They Help with Houseplant Gnats?

Zevo cartridges catch hundreds of adult fungus gnats navigating between indoor plants. However, fungus gnat larvae reside in the top 2 inches of moist potting soil. To completely eradicate them, let plant soil dry out between waterings and pair with Zevo's specialized Plant stake traps.

Which Zevo Refill Cartridge Fits Your Trap?

The most common mistake buyers make is ordering the wrong cartridge size. Always verify your device model before purchasing:

  • Zevo Standard & MAX Traps: Use the standard full-size refill cartridge (Model M364 / M45RF). These cartridges are 100% interchangeable between the original standard plug-in trap and the larger Zevo MAX trap.
  • Zevo Compact Traps: Utilize a distinct, ultra-mini cartridge designed for low-profile single-outlet plugs. Full-size cartridges will not fit.
  • Zevo Plant Traps: Feature specialized narrow adhesive stakes that insert into indoor plant soil. They require official Zevo Plant refills.

Tips for Getting Maximum Results from Your Zevo Trap

Even the strongest adhesive cartridge will underperform if the trap is placed in an ineffective location. Follow these four practical guidelines to maximize catch rates:

1. Place Near High-Activity Pathways

Position the trap in outlets where you frequently observe flying insects: near kitchen garbage cans, above recycling bins, adjacent to fruit bowls, or beside indoor potted plants. Keep the line of sight open—avoid hiding the trap behind thick furniture or drapes where insects cannot see the light.

2. Minimize Competing Light Sources

Zevo's blue and UV LEDs work most effectively when they are the primary light source in the immediate room. At night, keep bright overhead kitchen lights off so the trap stands out like a beacon to nocturnal gnats and flies.

3. Eliminate Moisture and Food Attractions

A trap only captures adult insects. To stop new generations from emerging, store ripe fruits in the refrigerator, rinse soda cans before tossing them in recycling, and regularly pour boiling water or enzyme cleaner down garbage disposals.

4. Inspect the Cartridge Regularly

Dust, cooking grease, and accumulated bugs gradually cover the adhesive surface. Replace the cartridge when the sticky area is roughly 75% full, or roughly every 30 to 45 days in active summer months.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Zevo refill cartridges work without the plug-in device?

No. The cartridge contains only the adhesive board and housing. It requires the Zevo plug-in base unit to emit the blue and UV light wavelengths that attract flying insects.

Do Zevo refills fit every Zevo trap?

No. The standard full-size refills fit standard Model 3/4 and Zevo MAX traps. Compact traps and Plant stake units require their own specific smaller refills.

Are Zevo refill cartridges scented?

No. Zevo standard cartridges are completely unscented with no artificial fragrances or toxic chemical baits. They rely entirely on optical light attraction.

Do the cartridges kill insects?

The high-tack adhesive permanently traps insects on contact. Once stuck, they cannot escape and dehydrate quickly on the glue surface.

How can I tell whether my Zevo cartridge is full?

Look through the clear inspection window on the back of the cartridge. If the glue is densely covered in bugs or has accumulated household dust, it is time for a fresh refill.

Can Zevo traps eliminate a fruit fly infestation?

They rapidly eliminate flying adult fruit flies, but they must be paired with source elimination (cleaning drains and disposing of overripe produce) to prevent new larvae from hatching.

Are Zevo refill cartridges worth buying in bulk?

Yes. Purchasing the 4-pack twin pack or larger bundles significantly reduces the cost per cartridge, ensuring uninterrupted protection throughout spring and summer.
