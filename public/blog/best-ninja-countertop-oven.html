Ninja Countertop Oven Buying Guide: What Should You Consider?

Before choosing a Ninja countertop oven, think about how you actually cook. A model with more features is not automatically the best option for every household.

1. Cooking Capacity

Capacity should be one of your first considerations. If you regularly prepare meals for several people, an XL model like the DT551 or DT501 may be more practical. If you mainly cook snacks, toast, or smaller portions, a compact flip-style oven like the SP151 may be sufficient. Consider the size of your usual baking trays, pizza, and meals before purchasing.

2. Cooking Functions

Different Ninja models offer different cooking programs. Common functions include:

  • Air Fry: For crisping food with high-velocity circulating hot air and minimal oil
  • Bake: For cakes, cookies, casseroles, and delicate baked treats
  • Roast: For vegetables, whole poultry, and roasted cuts of meat
  • Broil: For melting cheeses and browning toppings
  • Toast & Bagel: For everyday breakfast items with customizable shade levels
  • Dehydrate: For creating fruit chips, dried herbs, and jerky

3. Countertop Space

Measure your available kitchen space before ordering. Remember to consider the appliance's height, width, depth, door clearance, ventilation requirements, and any additional clearance needed for a flip-up design. A larger oven may offer more flexibility, but it also occupies more permanent counter space.

4. Thermometer and Smart Features

If you frequently prepare roasts or meat dishes, the DT551's integrated Pro Cook thermometer may be useful. If you rarely cook those foods, the DT501 may provide the functionality you need without that additional feature. For households preparing two different dishes simultaneously, the DCT801BK's dual-oven design may be more valuable than a built-in thermometer.

5. Cleaning and Accessories

Check which accessories come with the exact model you're considering. Air fry baskets, sheet pans, wire racks, and removable crumb trays can make everyday cooking and cleanup easier. Always follow the manufacturer's cleaning instructions, especially for nonstick or coated components.

6. Electrical Compatibility

Before buying, check the voltage, frequency, wattage, and plug type for your country. This is particularly important when ordering a US-market Ninja oven internationally. For example, some US models are rated for 120V and 60Hz. They should not be connected directly to a 220–240V supply without an appropriate, properly rated solution.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Ninja countertop ovens worth buying?

They can be worthwhile if you want one appliance that handles several cooking tasks. Their value depends on how frequently you use the different functions and whether the capacity fits your household.

What is the difference between Ninja DT551 and DT501?

Both models offer 10 cooking functions, Smart Surround Convection, and two-level cooking. The DT551 adds an integrated Pro Cook thermometer, making it more suitable for people who regularly prepare roasts and meat dishes.

Is the Ninja Double Oven better than a regular countertop oven?

It offers a different advantage: two separate cooking sections that can operate at different temperatures and times. This is useful when preparing multiple dishes simultaneously, but a traditional XL oven may be simpler for people who usually cook one item at a time.

Is the Ninja Flip SP151 suitable for a small kitchen?

It can be a practical option because its flip-up design helps reduce the countertop footprint when stored. You should still check the required clearance and storage space before purchasing.

Can a Ninja countertop oven replace a regular oven?

For many smaller meals, snacks, baking tasks, and air-frying needs, it may reduce how often you use a full-size oven. However, capacity limitations mean it may not replace a conventional oven for every household or large cooking task.
