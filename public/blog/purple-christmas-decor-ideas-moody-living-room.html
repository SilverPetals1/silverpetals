Best Purple Christmas Decor Ideas for a Moody Holiday Living Room

H
Written by SilverPetals • October 20, 2026 • 12 min read

Purple Christmas decor is a stunning way to create a holiday living room that feels luxurious, cozy, and refreshingly unexpected. Instead of relying on traditional red and green, this color palette brings together rich plum, deep aubergine, soft lavender, antique gold, and natural evergreen to create a space that feels dramatic without losing its festive warmth.

Whether you love dark, romantic interiors or simply want a Christmas tree that stands apart from standard holiday themes, purple offers boundless decorating possibilities. Deep purple velvet adds a tactile, couture touch, glass ornaments reflect the golden glow of fairy lights, and antique metallic accents make the entire living room feel regal and inviting.

The secret to making purple Christmas decorations look elegant is to balance rich jewel tones with warm 2700K lighting, organic foliage textures, and a few carefully chosen brass or gold details. You don't need to replace every decoration you own. A handful of deep plum baubles, a wired velvet ribbon, or aubergine throw pillows can transform your existing setup into a showstopping holiday sanctuary — explore our curated top picks on Amazon here.

Designer Styling Tip

When decorating with deep purple and plum, always prioritize warm-white lighting (2500K–2700K). Cool lighting can make dark purple appear stark or black, while warm golden illumination brings out the lustrous red and violet undertones in velvet ribbons and glass ornaments.

1. Decorate Your Christmas Tree With Deep Purple Ornaments

Target Budget: $20–$45 per set

The Christmas tree is the definitive centerpiece to introduce a dramatic purple color scheme. Deep plum, royal aubergine, and rich blackberry ornaments create breathtaking optical depth against the lush green pine branches of a traditional evergreen tree. Instead of one flat tone, combining glossy baubles with matte globes and textured glitter creates a layered designer look that catches fairy lights beautifully.

What to Look For:

  • Rich plum, deep aubergine, and royal purple colorway with jewel-tone saturation
  • Assorted textural finishes (mirror gloss, frosted matte, and micro-glitter)
  • Shatterproof construction safe around playful pets and young children
  • Sturdy hanging caps with pre-attached color-coordinated cords
  • Varied sizes (2.36'' to 3.15'') for balanced crown-to-skirt distribution

How to Style It:

Distribute the largest matte aubergine baubles deeper inside the inner branches to absorb light and create optical depth. Hang glossy and glittered plum ornaments near branch tips where warm-white fairy lights reflect off their surfaces. Pair with antique-gold baubles and a deep plum velvet ribbon cascade for royal elegance.

Why It Looks Expensive:

Deep jewel-tone saturation and contrasting surface finishes mimic artisanal mouth-blown glass ornaments, lending European estate grandeur without the fragile breakage risk.

Key Advantages

  • Shatterproof safety prevents messy breaks on hardwood and tile floors
  • Stunning mix of 4 unique light-reflecting textural finishes
  • Deep plum and aubergine tones do not look childish or neon
  • Generous multi-piece box dresses medium to large tree sections easily

Good to Know

  • Glitter baubles may shed slight micro-sparkles during initial branch hanging

2. Add Plum Velvet Ribbon to Your Christmas Tree

Target Budget: $12–$28 per roll

Velvet ribbon is one of the easiest, most cost-effective ways to make holiday decor look bespoke and high-end. Its dense, light-absorbing pile gives deep plum and aubergine hues a multidimensional, tactile richness that standard satin or organza ribbon can never achieve. Wired edges ensure bows stay crisp, voluminous, and perfectly arched throughout the festive season.

What to Look For:

  • Sumptuous, heavyweight velvet fabric with silky micro-pile
  • Concealed flexible wire sewn cleanly into both fabric borders
  • Deep plum-wine tone that harmonizes with antique brass, pine, and cream
  • Holds dramatic sculptural loops and cascading waterfalls without drooping
  • Continuous 10-yard spool provides ample material for large bow projects

How to Style It:

Create three to five oversized 4-loop statement bows and place them at staggered heights throughout your tree. Let long, curling tails billow loosely down through the branches. Echo the ribbon by tying smaller velvet accents onto your staircase garland and your front door wreath to unify the entire ground floor.

Why It Looks Expensive:

Heavy velvet absorbs ambient light while casting soft shadows, creating that moody, romantic Old World atmosphere celebrated in luxury holiday design showcases.

Key Advantages

  • Wired edges hold dramatic bows and looping cascades effortlessly

  • Reusable for seasons to come without fraying

Good to Know

More Moody Purple Holiday Styling Ideas

11. Hang Oversized Purple Bows on Wreaths and Greenery

Oversized statement bows are an effortless way to make a Christmas display feel dramatic and intentional. A large plum velvet bow secured to an evergreen wreath creates a captivating focal point without requiring dozens of individual baubles. Repeating the same bow on your fireplace garland or staircase balusters unifies your living room with tailored rhythm.

  • Choose wide 2.5'' to 4'' velvet or wired ribbon in deep plum or dark aubergine.
  • Attach one large statement bow to the top apex or bottom drop of an evergreen wreath.
  • Repeat smaller coordinating bow accents along nearby mantel garland loops.
  • Tuck small antique gold bells or brass sprigs into the bow knot for contrast.

12. Use Warm Lighting to Make Purple Decor Feel Cozy

Lighting can make or break a moody Christmas living room. Deep purple decorations can appear flat or almost black in poorly lit corners, while harsh cool lighting washes out rich velvet pile. Warm-white fairy lights (2700K), soft ceramic table lamps, and safe LED wax pillars create a golden glow that reveals the rich violet and wine undertones in your decor.

  • Stick strictly to warm-white fairy lights (2500K–2700K) on the tree and mantel.
  • Layer lighting at three distinct heights: tree lights, eye-level floor/table lamps, and low candle glow.
  • Keep overhead chandeliers dimmed to establish a relaxed, cozy evening ambiance.

13. Decorate With Purple and Antique Brass Accessories

Antique brass is the ultimate companion for deep purple because its muted, unlacquered warmth softens the intensity of dark jewel tones. Brass candlesticks, rustic harmony bells, decorative trays, and vintage bowl pedestals make the room feel collected, historical, and timeless rather than newly mass-produced.

  • Place staggered brass taper candleholders across the fireplace mantel.
  • Use an antique brass tray to organize holiday accessories and pinecones on the coffee table.
  • Bridge the colors with warm brushed gold ornaments on the Christmas tree.

14. Style a Purple Christmas Coffee Table Centerpiece

Your coffee table offers an intimate opportunity to bring holiday magic to eye level. A simple vignette of purple glass baubles, fresh cedar clippings, flickering LED candles, and an antique tray looks polished and inviting while leaving ample room for holiday drinks and books.

  • Start with a dark wood, matte black, or brushed brass decorative tray.
  • Nestle a low bowl of plum, violet, and crackle-glass baubles on one side.
  • Add a small vase with cedar sprigs and two glowing votives for flickering warmth.

15. Finish the Room With Purple Christmas Gift Wrapping

Gift wrapping is an essential component of professional holiday styling. Stacking gifts wrapped in coordinating plum, deep aubergine, matte cream, and kraft paper extends the holiday palette across the floor. Tied with plush velvet ribbon and antique gold gift tags, packages become an extension of your Christmas decor.

  • Wrap primary packages in neutral kraft paper and warm cream sheets.
  • Use rich plum paper for two or three statement gift boxes under the tree.
  • Finish every box with 1-inch plum velvet ribbon and natural jute or brass gift tags.

Budget-Friendly Tips for a Moody Holiday Living Room

  • Reuse your existing greenery: A classic green artificial tree and faux cedar garland serve as the ideal base for purple ornaments and ribbons.
  • Invest in one standout textile: A pair of plum velvet pillow covers or a 10-yard spool of wired velvet ribbon transforms the room for under $30.
  • Group ornaments strategically: A stylishly filled bowl of plum baubles on the coffee table often makes a stronger visual impact than scattering baubles sparsely across a huge tree.
  • Use lighting to elevate budget pieces: Inexpensive shatterproof ornaments look like heirloom glass when illuminated by warm 2700K fairy lights.
  • Choose multi-season accessories: Plum velvet cushions, brass candleholders, and dark ceramic trays can remain out through winter until spring arrives.

Common Mistakes to Avoid With Purple Christmas Decor

  • Using too many competing shades: Stick to one hero tone (e.g., plum) and one supporting tone (e.g., lavender) rather than blending every shade equally.
  • Overusing shiny metallics: Choose either antique gold OR silver as your primary metallic anchor to prevent visual chaos.
  • Cool white lighting: Avoid harsh 5000K daylight LEDs, which wash out velvet pile and turn aubergine into flat black.
  • Forgetting texture: Layer velvet, matte resin, mercury glass, and natural evergreen to keep the space tactile and dynamic.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is purple a good color for Christmas decorations?

Yes! Purple offers a refreshing, sophisticated alternative to traditional red and green. It works exceptionally well in elegant, vintage-inspired, glamorous, and moody holiday living rooms. Deep plum and aubergine feel luxurious and warm, while lavender and violet create a frosty, modern winter atmosphere.

What colors go best with purple Christmas decor?

Purple pairs beautifully with antique gold, emerald green, champagne brass, silver, warm cream, charcoal, and natural wood tones. Antique gold and forest green create an opulent traditional atmosphere, while silver and clear glass produce a cool, frosty contemporary look.

How can I make purple Christmas decorations look expensive?

Use a disciplined color palette of two or three harmonious shades, combine contrasting textures (such as plush velvet, mercury glass, and burnished brass), prioritize warm 2700K lighting, and repeat your key accent color across the tree, sofa, and mantel rather than scattering mismatched items.

Does purple Christmas decor work in a small living room?

Absolutely. Focus on high-impact focal points such as a beautifully decorated tree, two plum velvet throw cushions on the sofa, and a simple bowl of glass baubles on the coffee table. Balance darker jewel tones with light cream textiles to keep the room feeling open and welcoming.

Should I choose plum, lavender, or aubergine for Christmas?

Choose deep plum if you want a rich, cozy, and welcoming holiday setting; aubergine for a dark, dramatic, and romantic atmosphere; and soft lavender if you prefer a bright, frosty, and modern winter aesthetic. Your choice should complement your existing upholstery and wall colors.

Can I combine purple Christmas decorations with traditional green?

Yes! Natural evergreen pine and cedar are the easiest, most natural companions for purple. A green tree decorated with plum ornaments, wired velvet ribbon, and subtle antique gold touches feels deeply festive without looking overly commercial.

What is the easiest way to try purple Christmas decor on a budget?

Start with small, high-impact changes: swap existing throw cushion covers for plum velvet covers, wrap wired plum ribbon through your existing evergreen tree, or fill a decorative tabletop bowl with a box of assorted purple baubles.

Final Thoughts

Purple Christmas decor is an inspired design choice if you crave a holiday living room that feels dramatic, cozy, and refreshingly distinct from conventional holiday themes. From deep plum baubles and aubergine velvet stockings to warm antique gold candleholders, there are countless ways to craft a rich, unforgettable holiday haven.

Begin by dressing your tree in deep plum ornaments and wired velvet ribbon, add coordinating velvet pillows to your sofa, and introduce glowing candles along the mantelpiece. With thoughtful textures, warm lighting, and a balanced jewel-tone palette, your living room will feel like an inviting, festive sanctuary all season long.
