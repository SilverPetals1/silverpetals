Camp Chef Woodwind Pro Review: Is This Premium Pellet Grill Worth Buying?

S
Written by SilverPetals • November 17, 2026 • 13 min read

If you enjoy authentic barbecue but want more convenience than a traditional offset smoker, the Camp Chef Woodwind Pro deserves a closer look. This premium pellet grill combines automatic temperature control with a feature designed to solve one of the biggest complaints about pellet smokers: limited smoke flavor.

Its standout feature is the built-in Smoke Box, which allows you to burn real wood chunks, wood chips, or charcoal alongside the regular wood pellets. This gives you more control over smoke intensity while keeping the convenience of a pellet-fed cooking system.

But is the Camp Chef Woodwind Pro worth its premium price? In this comprehensive review, we'll explore its features, cooking performance, advantages, disadvantages, and the type of buyer who may benefit most from this smoker.

Camp Chef Woodwind Pro: Product Overview

The Camp Chef Woodwind Pro is a Wi-Fi-enabled pellet grill designed for smoking, roasting, and outdoor barbecue cooking. Unlike conventional pellet grills that rely primarily on wood pellets for heat and smoke, the Pro adds a separate Smoke Box for additional wood-fired flavor.

It has been offered in different sizes, including 24-inch and 36-inch configurations. Availability and exact specifications depend on the model and retailer.

1. Smoke Box: The Main Reason to Consider the Woodwind Pro

The Smoke Box is the most distinctive feature of the Camp Chef Woodwind Pro.

Traditional pellet grills burn hardwood pellets inside a firepot to generate heat and smoke. Although this system offers convenience, some barbecue enthusiasts feel that pellet grills don't always deliver the same smoke intensity as traditional wood-burning smokers.

Camp Chef addresses this with a separate compartment positioned near the firepot. You can add:

  • Hardwood chunks (hickory, apple, pecan, oak)
  • Wood chips
  • Additional wood pellets
  • Lump charcoal

The additional fuel creates more smoke while the pellet system continues managing the cooking temperature.

Wood-fired flavor made easy: multi-tier rack smoking ribs, chickens, and artisan pizzas

Does the Smoke Box Make a Difference?

Independent testing by AmazingRibs found that the Smoke Box was particularly useful for adding stronger smoke flavor during medium- and higher-temperature cooking. Its tests also found that the difference was less noticeable during some long, low-and-slow cooks.

This is an important distinction: the Smoke Box is not necessarily something every barbecue recipe requires. Its biggest appeal is giving users more control over smoke intensity.

Why it matters: If you enjoy experimenting with different woods and want more smoke flavor than a conventional pellet grill provides, this feature may justify considering the Woodwind Pro.

2. Temperature Control and Wi-Fi Connectivity

A major advantage of pellet grills is their ability to automate much of the temperature-management process. The Woodwind Pro includes a digital controller and Wi-Fi connectivity, allowing compatible smartphones to connect through the Camp Chef Connect app.

Depending on the supported features, users can:

  • Monitor cooking temperatures live from anywhere
  • Adjust cooking settings remotely
  • Check food temperature probes in real-time
  • Manage smoke level settings
  • Track long multi-hour smoking sessions

Is Wi-Fi Actually Useful?

For long smoking sessions, remote monitoring can be extraordinarily convenient. Instead of repeatedly walking outside to check the controller, you can monitor supported cooking information through your phone.

However, Wi-Fi does not eliminate the need to check your food, manage fuel, or maintain the grill. It is a convenience feature rather than a replacement for proper barbecue technique.

4. Down & Out Ventilation System

The Woodwind Pro uses Camp Chef's Down & Out ventilation design. Instead of relying on a conventional chimney arrangement, the system uses adjustable rear exhaust vents to guide heat and smoke through the cooking chamber.

The idea is to encourage smoke circulation around the food before it exits the grill. This design works alongside the Smoke Box and adjustable smoke settings to give users more control over the cooking environment. For barbecue enthusiasts, smoke circulation is important because the way smoke travels through a cooking chamber can influence the flavor and consistency of the final food.

5. Adjustable Smoke Settings

Another useful feature is the adjustable Smoke Number system. The controller provides different smoke settings (from 1 to 10), allowing users to select their preferred smoke intensity. This is helpful because different recipes benefit from different amounts of smoke:

  • Chicken: Benefits from a moderate smoke profile to avoid overpowering delicate poultry.
  • Brisket: Can be prepared with a longer smoking session and deep bark development.
  • Ribs: Cooked with a combination of controlled heat and supplemental wood smoke.
  • Vegetables: Exposed to lighter smoke for a balanced aroma.

6. Cleaning and Maintenance

Cleaning is an important consideration when buying a premium pellet grill. The Woodwind Pro includes Camp Chef's ash-cleanout system, which allows accumulated ash to be collected through a removable cup beneath the firepot. This can make routine maintenance easier than manually reaching into the firepot every time ash needs to be removed.

However, the system does not mean the grill is maintenance-free. You should still:

  • Empty the ash collection cup regularly after long smokes.
  • Clean grease from the drip tray and grease bucket.
  • Remove food residue from cooking grates with a brass brush.
  • Inspect the firepot periodically for unburned pellets.
  • Check the pellet hopper for moisture or pellet dust.
  • Keep the temperature probes clean.
  • Follow the manufacturer's instructions for emptying the Smoke Box ash.

7. Build Quality and Design

The Woodwind Pro uses a combination of powder-coated steel and stainless-steel components, depending on the section and model. Its overall design is intended for regular outdoor cooking rather than occasional portable use.

The larger Woodwind Pro 36 weighs approximately 183 pounds in the reviewed configuration, making it a substantial appliance that requires a suitable outdoor location. Its construction, storage shelf, cooking racks, and integrated hopper create a dedicated backyard barbecue setup.

Is the Camp Chef Woodwind Pro Good for Brisket?

The Woodwind Pro can be an interesting option for brisket because it combines automatic temperature management with additional smoke-generation options. A long brisket cook requires stable temperatures, adequate cooking space, and enough fuel for an extended session.

The larger 36-inch version may be particularly useful when preparing multiple cuts or cooking for a group. However, remember that the Smoke Box is not a substitute for good brisket technique. Meat selection, seasoning, temperature management, resting time, and proper food handling still matter.

Is the Camp Chef Woodwind Pro Worth the Money?

Whether the Woodwind Pro is worth buying depends on what you expect from a pellet grill. If you primarily want a convenient appliance for occasional burgers, chicken, and basic smoking, a less expensive pellet grill may be a better purchase.

But if smoke flavor is one of your biggest priorities, the Woodwind Pro offers something that many conventional pellet grills do not: a dedicated compartment for burning additional wood fuel. That feature makes it especially interesting for barbecue enthusiasts who enjoy experimenting with different smoking techniques.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes the Camp Chef Woodwind Pro different from other pellet grills?

Its integrated Smoke Box is the main difference. It allows users to burn wood chunks, chips, pellets, or charcoal separately from the main pellet fire, providing additional smoke options.

Can you use charcoal in the Camp Chef Woodwind Pro?

Yes. The Smoke Box is designed to accept charcoal as well as different types of wood fuel. Follow Camp Chef's instructions for suitable fuel and operation.

Does the Camp Chef Woodwind Pro have Wi-Fi?

Yes. The Woodwind Pro includes Wi-Fi connectivity and supports the Camp Chef Connect app for compatible smartphones.

How large is the Camp Chef Woodwind Pro 36?

The Woodwind Pro 36 has been listed with approximately 1,236 square inches of total cooking space, including its upper and lower racks. Verify the exact specifications of the listing before purchasing.

Is the Smoke Box necessary for every cook?

No. The grill can operate as a conventional pellet smoker without relying on additional fuel in the Smoke Box. The extra smoke feature is optional and can be used when you want a different smoke profile.

Can the Camp Chef Woodwind Pro cold-smoke food?

The grill includes a Fan Only mode designed to support cold-smoking applications. However, maintaining a safe and suitable temperature can be difficult, particularly in warm weather. Cold smoking meat and fish carries serious food-safety risks and should not be attempted without a validated process.

Final Verdict: Should You Buy the Camp Chef Woodwind Pro?

The Camp Chef Woodwind Pro is an interesting premium pellet grill because it addresses a genuine limitation that some pellet-grill owners experience: getting stronger wood-smoke flavor.

Its integrated Smoke Box, digital temperature controls, Wi-Fi connectivity, multiple cooking racks, and convenient ash-cleanout system make it a versatile option for serious backyard barbecue enthusiasts.

The biggest reason to choose it over a standard pellet grill is the additional smoke control. If that feature matters to you, the Woodwind Pro deserves a place on your shortlist.

Our recommendation: Consider the Woodwind Pro if you regularly smoke meat, enjoy experimenting with different wood fuels, and are comfortable investing in a premium outdoor cooking appliance.
