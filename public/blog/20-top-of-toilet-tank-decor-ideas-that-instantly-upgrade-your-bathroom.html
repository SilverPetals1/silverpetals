20 Top of Toilet Tank Decor Ideas That Instantly Upgrade Your Bathroom

H
Written by SilverPetals • October 2, 2026 • 10 min read

Turn an overlooked utilitarian porcelain lid into a polished, intentional design moment with smart, functional styling accents.

1. A Small Tray to Corral a Few Curated Items

A decorative tray is the single most effective design foundation for styling a toilet tank lid. Corrals prevent individual objects from looking scattered or accidental, framing your decorative pieces into one cohesive visual vignette. Trays also make bathroom cleaning effortless, allowing you to lift the entire collection in one swift motion when wiping down porcelain surfaces.

Styling Tip: Choose a shallow tray in marble, woven rattan, or matte brass that fits comfortably within the lid dimensions without hanging over the beveled ceramic edges.

2. A Single Bud Vase with a Fresh or Faux Stem

Introducing a delicate bud vase brings vertical elegance and organic life to an otherwise stark porcelain plane. Because the surface is compact, a single sculptural flower or a faux olive branch delivers vibrant color and gentle movement without crowding the space. It adds an understated softness that immediately elevates powder rooms and guest baths.

Styling Tip: Opt for a heavy ceramic, fluted glass, or stoneware vase with a wide, weighted base so it stays completely steady during day-to-day use.

3. A Small Stack of Folded Hand Towels

Placing neatly folded waffle-knit or plush Turkish cotton hand towels on top of the tank communicates welcoming hospitality. It provides guests with immediate access to fresh linens while introducing soft, tactile fabric texture against hard bathroom fixtures. It bridges practical utility and spa-like luxury effortlessly.

Styling Tip: Fold two or three linen fingertip towels into uniform thirds and rest them neatly inside a shallow ceramic dish or wooden tray to protect them from moisture.

4. A Reed Diffuser for Subtle, Continuous Fragrance

A reed diffuser provides continuous, flame-free aromatic luxury that keeps your bathroom smelling fresh around the clock. Unlike aerosols or candles, natural rattan reeds gently disperse calming scents like white tea, eucalyptus, or crisp bergamot without requiring active supervision or electricity.

Styling Tip: Choose an amber or smoked glass apothecary bottle that complements your fixture hardware, and flip the reeds once a week to maintain a subtle, welcoming fragrance throw.

5. A Small Potted Plant Suited to Bathroom Humidity

Live plants naturally soften sterile bathroom porcelain and thrive on the ambient humidity created by daily showers. Compact varieties like a bird's nest fern, snake plant, or small pothos inject vibrant green tones and organic energy, purifying indoor air while creating a calming indoor garden effect.

Styling Tip: Place your humidity-loving potted plant inside a matte glazed ceramic or terracotta cachepot with rubber feet to protect the toilet lid from water rings.

6. A Decorative Soap Dish for Guest Hand Soap

In powder rooms where vanity counter space is limited, styling a luxurious artisanal soap dish atop the toilet tank serves as both charming decor and a thoughtful guest convenience. An organic French-milled soap bar wrapped in textured paper or embossed with botanical details looks delightfully refined.

Styling Tip: Select a self-draining ridged marble, travertine, or fluted glass dish to prevent soap residue from accumulating and keep the surface impeccably clean.

7. A Small Candle for Ambiance

A scented candle housed in a heavy ceramic or frosted glass jar brings warm ambiance and olfactory charm to the bathroom. Even kept unlit for safety, quality soy and beeswax candles gently release cold scent throws that keep the room subtly fragrant throughout the day.

Styling Tip: Pair your display candle with a decorative glass cloche or an antique brass snuffer to create a balanced, vintage-inspired vignette on your tank tray.

8. A Mini Bookend with One or Two Bathroom-Appropriate Books

Adding a curated pair of mini books held by a compact stone bookend infuses your bathroom with personality and relaxed charm. Design monographs, poetry collections, or travel essays bound in linen cloth bring intellectual character and tailored color to a traditionally utilitarian spot.

Styling Tip: Use a single weighty marble sphere or brass prism as a bookend, ensuring books rest safely away from water splash zones.

9. A Small Ceramic Dish for Extra Jewelry or Rings

A handcrafted ceramic trinket dish atop the toilet tank provides a dedicated safe harbor for wedding rings, watches, or earrings before handwashing or showering. Its small footprint adds artisanal pottery texture without taking up valuable vanity counter space.

Styling Tip: Choose an organic, imperfect wabi-sabi clay dish with reactive glaze details to introduce handmade warmth against pristine ceramic porcelain.

10. A Woven Basket for Rolled Washcloths

A small woven seagrass or rattan basket filled with tightly rolled white washcloths brings high-end luxury resort styling into your home. The woven basket introduces natural earthy texture while providing practical grab-and-go convenience for family members and visiting guests.

Styling Tip: Roll fresh cotton washcloths tightly and stand them vertically inside the basket to create a neat, uniform spa presentation.

11. A Small Framed Photo or Print

Leaning a small framed black-and-white photograph, botanical sketch, or architectural print against the wall behind your toilet tank personalizes the space in seconds. It bridges the vertical gap between the tank lid and overhead shelving or mirrors, creating a deliberate designer focal point.

Styling Tip: Use water-resistant metal or sealed wood frames with moisture-proof acrylic glazing instead of delicate antique paper backings that could warp from steam.

12. A Textured Stone or Ceramic Object as a Simple Accent

If you prefer an uncluttered, modern look, a single textured sculpture—such as an interlocking marble chain link, a volcanic stone knot, or a ceramic donut ring—makes a bold, minimalist statement. It transforms the toilet tank into a museum-worthy design pedestal without functional clutter.

Styling Tip: Keep the surrounding lid surface completely bare to allow the sculptural object's silhouettes and raw textures to command visual attention.

13. A Small Jar of Cotton Balls or Bath Salts

Clear apothecary jars filled with white cotton balls, swabs, or Epsom bath salts combine hygienic storage with visual charm. The transparent glass catches natural window light, reflecting cleanliness and everyday practicality while keeping essential grooming supplies within easy reach.

Styling Tip: Select jars with airtight silicone gaskets and brass or turned wooden lids to keep cotton pads dry and protected from bathroom steam.

14. A Mini Succulent or Air Plant for Low-Maintenance Greenery

For bathrooms with minimal natural sunlight or homeowners with busy schedules, a miniature succulent or tillandsia air plant offers vibrant green life with almost zero maintenance. Their sculptural forms and petite root systems fit perfectly into tiny ceramic bowls or geometric brass holders.

Styling Tip: Place an air plant inside an open geometric wire stand so airflow reaches the leaves, giving it a quick weekly misting to keep it lush.

15. A Decorative Box for Extra Toiletries

A decorative lidded box in bone inlay, fluted wood, or woven cane provides clever, discreet storage for extra personal toiletries, matchbooks, or flushable wipes. It completely conceals everyday bathroom necessities, keeping the porcelain lid tidy and elegant.

Styling Tip: Choose a box with a secure hinged or fitted lid so items remain invisible and completely shielded from dust and moisture.

16. A Small Sculptural Object to Add Visual Interest

Introducing a small decorative bust, carved soapstone figurine, or geometric brass polyhedron infuses artful sophistication into your bathroom decor. It challenges the purely utilitarian perception of a toilet tank, turning it into an intentional curation zone that reflects personal taste.

Styling Tip: Anchor the sculptural piece on top of a small stone coaster or stack of two vintage paperbacks to give it intentional height and presence.

17. A Single Stem of Eucalyptus or Dried Botanicals

Preserved silver dollar eucalyptus, bunny tails, or dried lavender stems offer long-lasting botanical elegance that never wilts or requires watering. The natural oils in eucalyptus gently react with warm shower steam, releasing subtle herbal aromas that calm the senses during bath rituals.

Styling Tip: Trim stems so they stand no taller than twelve inches, preventing top-heavy arrangements from tipping over during daily bathroom routines.

18. A Tiny Tissue Box Cover for a Cohesive Look

Standard branded cardboard tissue boxes create visual noise that disrupts cohesive bathroom palettes. Encasing facial tissues inside a cube cover crafted from textured linen, brushed brass, or light oak transforms an everyday utility into a seamless designer accent.

Styling Tip: Coordinate the tissue box finish with your vanity cabinet hardware or faucet metals to tie the bathroom's design theme together effortlessly.

19. A Small Dish for Hair Ties or Bobby Pins

Keep stray bobby pins, silk scrunchies, and hair clips organized with a tiny fluted ceramic or scalloped marble pinch dish. It catches hair accessories right where you need them before washing up, eliminating loose pins scattered across vanity countertops and shower ledges.

Styling Tip: Place the hair dish alongside a mini candle on your corral tray for a charming, organized bedside-table aesthetic right on your tank lid.

20. A Minimal, Curated Arrangement of Just Two or Three Items for a Clean Look

When styling compact bathroom surfaces, restraint is always the ultimate mark of sophistication. A simple triad—such as a slim marble tray, a mini potted fern, and an amber glass reed diffuser—strikes the ideal balance between functional fragrance, living greenery, and clean visual calm.

Styling Tip: Use the rule of threes by varying the heights of your three chosen items (tall diffuser, medium plant, low tray) to achieve balanced visual harmony without clutter.

Elevate Your Bathroom with Mindful, Functional Styling

Styling the top of your toilet tank is a small design gesture that yields an enormous aesthetic return. By taking a few minutes to replace clutter or bare porcelain with a curated tray, gentle room fragrance, and organic natural textures, you bring intentional polish and boutique luxury into your daily routine. Remember that less is always more in a bathroom environment: prioritize moisture-resistant materials, keep arrangements stable, and ensure your styling never hinders easy cleaning or functional flushing. With these thoughtful touches, your bathroom will always feel serene, welcoming, and completely pulled together.
