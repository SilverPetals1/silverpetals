Breville's Element iQ technology is one of the important features that separates the Pizzaiolo from a basic countertop oven. This helps create different cooking environments for different pizza styles. For example, a thin pizza requires a different balance of top and bottom heat than a thicker pan pizza loaded with toppings. The system manages these differences automatically through preset cooking profiles.

Breville Pizzaiolo Buying Guide: What Should You Consider?

The Breville Pizzaiolo is a high-end specialized appliance. Consider these factors before making your purchase decision:

1. Pizza Size (12-Inch Limit)

The Pizzaiolo is designed specifically for pizzas up to 12 inches in diameter. If you frequently feed large crowds and want to bake 16-inch extra-large pizzas, you will need to prepare multiple pies consecutively rather than one giant pizza.

2. Kitchen Counter Space & Storage

At 37 pounds and approximately 18.5 inches deep, this is an appliance you will likely want to leave out on the counter. Make sure you have adequate clearance from overhead cabinets and combustible materials.

3. Preheating Time

Reaching 750°F requires approximately 20 minutes of preheating. Once preheated, however, pizzas bake in just 2 minutes, allowing you to turn out multiple hot pies quickly for family or guests.

4. Indoor vs Outdoor Cooking Convenience

Outdoor gas and wood-fired pizza ovens (such as Ooni or Gozney) are popular, but they require outdoor space, fuel tanks or pellets, and fair weather. The Pizzaiolo operates indoors year-round regardless of rain, snow, or wind.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Breville Pizzaiolo worth the money?

For dedicated pizza lovers who want authentic Neapolitan-style pizza at home without setting up an outdoor wood oven, it is one of the only electric countertop ovens capable of true 750°F baking. Its performance, build quality, and convenience justify the investment for regular pizza makers.

Can the Pizzaiolo be used for other foods?

Yes. While engineered primarily for pizza, it can roast vegetables, bake flatbreads, sear steaks on cast iron, and bake focaccia using its lower temperature settings and included pan.

How do you clean the pizza stone?

Allow the stone to cool completely. Scrape off baked-on debris with a dry brush or plastic scraper, and wipe with a damp cloth. Never submerge the cordierite stone in water or use dish soap, as porous stone absorbs moisture and chemicals.

Does it produce a lot of smoke indoors?

When used properly with moderate flour on the peel, smoke is minimal. Excessive loose flour on the stone can burn at 750°F, so using a perforated peel and semolina flour helps prevent excess flour from burning.
