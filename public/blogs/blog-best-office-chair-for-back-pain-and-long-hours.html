Best Office Chair for Back Pain and Long Hours: 6 Ergonomic Picks Worth Considering

H
Written by SilverPetals • September 22, 2026 • 12 min read

Sitting at a desk for eight or more hours can become uncomfortable very quickly when your chair does not properly support your body. A chair that is too low, too deep, too stiff, or missing useful lumbar support can leave you constantly changing positions just to stay comfortable.

That is why choosing the best office chair for back pain and long hours is less about buying the chair with the thickest cushion and more about finding one that can be adjusted to your body.

For this guide, we compared popular ergonomic office chairs based on lumbar support, seat adjustment, armrest flexibility, recline, materials, weight capacity, warranty, and suitability for extended desk work.

Our overall pick is the Steelcase Leap because of its extensive adjustability, flexible back support, adjustable lumbar system, and seat-depth control. However, there are several good alternatives depending on your budget and preferred sitting style.
