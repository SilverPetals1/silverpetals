How to Choose the Best Chair for a Small Space

Choosing a chair for a small room requires more than finding an attractive design. You need to consider its actual dimensions, movement clearance, comfort, and how it fits with your existing furniture.

Here are the most important factors to consider.

1. Measure the Available Floor Space

Before buying a chair, measure the area where you intend to place it. Check the overall chair width, overall chair depth, chair height, seat width and depth, and the space needed to move around it comfortably.

Remember that the chair's listed width and depth do not tell the whole story. A swivel chair needs room to rotate, while a recliner requires additional clearance behind it.

2. Choose Armless or Slim-Arm Designs

Traditional armchairs can take up more space because their armrests extend beyond the seat. Armless chairs and slim-arm models can be easier to fit between other furniture pieces and create a more open appearance, especially in narrow living rooms.

3. Consider Lightweight and Open Frames

Furniture with exposed legs and open frames can make a room feel less crowded. Instead of choosing a fully enclosed chair with a large upholstered base, consider designs with raised legs or visible frame construction that allow more of the floor to remain visible.

4. Look for Multifunctional Features

Multifunctional furniture is particularly useful in small apartments. Consider chairs with hidden storage, folding chairs for occasional guests, swivel chairs for flexible seating, or compact chairs with removable cushions.

5. Think About Cushioning and Back Support

A compact chair should still be comfortable. Check the seat depth, cushion thickness, backrest height, and overall seating position. Try to balance comfort with the available footprint.

6. Select the Right Upholstery

The upholstery affects both the appearance and maintenance requirements of a chair. Common choices include linen-style fabrics for a relaxed natural look, velvet for soft luxury, bouclé for texture, and faux leather for easy wipe-down cleaning.

7. Match the Chair to Your Room's Layout

Try to position the chair where it supports the existing layout rather than interrupting it. A small accent chair can fit beside a sofa, while a slipper chair may work better in a narrow corner.

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Buying Chairs for Small Spaces

  • Buying Based Only on Product Photos: Product images can make furniture look smaller than it actually is. Always check the published dimensions before ordering.
  • Ignoring the Chair's Depth: A chair may be narrow but still extend far into the room, blocking walking paths.
  • Choosing Oversized Armrests: Wide armrests can increase the overall footprint without adding seating area.
  • Forgetting Movement Clearance: A chair should not block doors, drawers, or main walkways. Swivel models need additional turning room.
  • Prioritizing Appearance Over Comfort: A stylish chair is not a good purchase if its seat is too shallow or its backrest lacks support.
  • Buying Furniture Without Checking Assembly: Some chairs require extensive assembly and need workspace during setup.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the best type of chair for a small living room?

Armless accent chairs, slipper chairs, and compact mid-century designs are often good choices. They provide individual seating without the bulky appearance of oversized armchairs.

2. Are armless chairs better for small spaces?

Armless chairs can be useful because they eliminate the extra width of traditional armrests. However, they may provide less side support, so comfort should also be considered.

3. What is the most space-saving chair?

A folding chair is one of the most space-saving options because it can be stored when not in use. For permanent seating, a compact armless chair may be more suitable.

4. Can I put an accent chair in a small bedroom?

Yes. A compact accent chair can create a comfortable reading or dressing area. Measure the available floor space and make sure the chair does not block wardrobe doors or walking paths.

5. Are swivel chairs suitable for small apartments?

Yes, provided there is enough clearance for the chair to rotate. A swivel chair can be useful in multifunctional living spaces, but some models have a wider footprint than traditional accent chairs.

6. How do I make a small room look bigger with chairs?

Choose furniture with slim frames, exposed legs, lighter upholstery, and simple silhouettes. Avoid oversized designs that block walkways or cover too much visible floor space.

7. What chair color is best for a small room?

Neutral shades such as beige, cream, light gray, and soft taupe can blend easily with smaller interiors. However, a darker accent chair can also work beautifully when balanced with lighter walls and surrounding furniture.

8. Should I choose a chair with storage?

A storage chair can be useful if you need additional organization. It is particularly practical in small bedrooms and apartments, where separate storage furniture may take up valuable floor space.
