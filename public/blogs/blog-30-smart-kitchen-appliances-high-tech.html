30 Smart Kitchen Appliances to Make Your Home High-Tech

H
Written by SilverPetals • July 11, 2026 • 30 min read
Smart kitchen technology has evolved beyond simple gimmicks into genuinely useful, convenient upgrades that streamline your daily cooking routines and improve energy efficiency. From refrigerators that automatically track your grocery inventory to ovens that preheat remotely via voice assistant commands, these connected devices offer practical, time-saving benefits. This guide covers the 30 best smart kitchen appliances to transform your home into a high-tech culinary sanctuary.

1. Smart Refrigerator with Internal Cameras and Touchscreen

This high-tech refrigerator features built-in internal cameras that snapshot your shelves every time the door closes, letting you check your milk levels remotely from the grocery store. The large door touchscreen display allows you to manage family calendars, stream music, and find recipes based on remaining ingredients, offering ultimate convenience.

2. Wi-Fi-Connected Slow Cooker with App Control

Control your slow cooker meals remotely using an intuitive smartphone app. This connected appliance allows you to adjust temperature settings, monitor cooking times, and shift to a warm mode from your office, ensuring your stews are perfectly cooked and ready to serve when you arrive home.

3. Smart Oven with Remote Preheating and Monitoring

Preheat your oven while commuting home by using connected mobile apps or simple voice commands. Internal cameras let you monitor your roasting chicken on your phone screen, preventing overcooking and ensuring perfect meals without needing to open the oven door and lose heat.

4. Voice-Controlled Smart Faucet

This touchless faucet connects to voice assistants, letting you dispense precise amounts of water at specific temperatures. Command it to fill a baby bottle or dispense two cups of hot water, improving baking precision and kitchen hygiene when your hands are messy.

5. Smart Coffee Maker with Scheduling and App Brewing

Schedule your morning brew from your bed using app integrations. This coffee maker grinds beans and brews espresso automatically at your scheduled wake-up time, letting you customize cup strength and milk ratios for a coffee house experience at home.

6. Induction Cooktop with Smart Temperature Sensors

This smart induction cooktop uses wireless temperature probes to monitor pots, automatically adjusting power to prevent water boil-overs. It communicates directly with recipes to guide you through delicate candy making or steak searing, ensuring professional cooking results.

7. Smart Dishwasher with App Notifications

Monitor washing cycles and receive alerts on your smartphone when detergent is running low. This connected dishwasher optimizes water usage based on soil sensors and can be scheduled to run during off-peak energy hours, saving money and resources.

8. Under-Cabinet Smart Lighting with Voice Control

Adjust your kitchen lighting using voice assistants or app presets. These smart LED strips shift from bright white task lighting during prep to warm amber hues for dining, creating the perfect kitchen mood.

9. Smart Trash Can with Automatic Sensor Lid

Open your trash can touchlessly using voice commands or proximity sensors. This prevents the spread of bacteria when prepping raw meat, while internal odor-absorbing filters keep your kitchen smelling clean.

10. Precision Cookers (Sous Vide) with App-Guided Recipes

Cook restaurant-quality steaks with an app-connected immersion circulator. It maintains water temperature within fractions of a degree, while the app guides you through pre-programmed cooking times for perfect results.

11. Smart Water Filtration System with Usage Tracking

Track your daily water consumption and monitor filter health via app dashboards. This system alerts you when it is time to swap cartridges, ensuring clean drinking water.

12. App-Connected Multi-Cooker

This connected pressure cooker adjusts settings based on selected app recipes. It monitors pressure levels and notifies your phone when meals are ready, offering easy multi-tasking.

13. Smart Range Hood with Automatic Ventilation Sensors

This range hood uses heat and steam sensors to automatically turn on its fan when you start cooking, adjusting blower speed to clear smoke and keep air fresh.

14. Smart Plugs for Remote Appliance Control

Control small appliances like toasters or kettles remotely by using smart plugs. They let you cut power via mobile apps to prevent electrical hazards and save energy.

15. Smart Scale with Nutritional Tracking Integration

Weigh ingredients and track their nutritional value using an app-connected scale. The scale calculates calories, macronutrients, and vitamins as you prepare meals, helping you hit health goals.

16. Voice Assistant Hub Built Into Kitchen Display

Manage recipes and stream videos while cooking using an integrated voice assistant hub. The screen guides you through cooking steps and answers queries hands-free.

17. Smart Wine Fridge with App-Based Inventory Tracking

Track your wine collection using app-connected inventory systems. The fridge monitors bottle placements and temperature zones, helping you find the perfect bottle for dinner.

18. Robotic Vacuum with Kitchen-Specific Scheduling

Schedule your robot vacuum to clean the kitchen floor after dinner, keeping the room free of food crumbs without needing to sweep.

19. Smart Food Storage Containers with Freshness Tracking

Track food expiration dates by using app-connected storage lids, helping you reduce waste by alerting you when ingredients need to be used.

20. App-Controlled Smart Blinds for Kitchen Windows

Adjust window blinds remotely using app commands or smart sensors. The blinds open and close based on sun exposure to keep the room cool.

21. Smart Smoke and CO Detector Integrated with Kitchen Safety

Receive immediate phone alerts if smoke or gas leaks are detected. The sensor integrates with smart home systems to shut down gas ranges automatically in an emergency.

22. Touchless Smart Soap Dispenser

Dispense soap touchlessly using motion sensors. This prevents the spread of bacteria when cleaning up prep stations.

23. Smart Induction Warming Drawer

Keep cooked dishes warm at precise temperatures using app controls, helping you coordinate serving times when hosting.

24. App-Connected Sous Vide Immersion Circulator

Control cook times and water temperatures remotely using an app, ensuring restaurant-quality results every time.

25. Smart Pantry Inventory Tracker

Track pantry storage levels using app-connected scale pads, notifying your phone when you are running low on coffee or flour.

26. Smart Kitchen Display for Recipes

Stream cooking videos and manage ingredients using wall-mounted smart hubs, keeping you hands-free while preparing dinner.

27. Smart Garbage Disposal with Jam Detection

Automatic jam detection reverses blades if bones get stuck, protecting motor life and keeping cleanup running smoothly.

28. Under-Sink Smart Leak Detector

Receive immediate alerts if sink pipes start leaking, helping you prevent expensive water damage under base cabinetry.

29. Smart Kettle with App-Based Temperature Control

Heat tea water to precise temperatures using app controls, ensuring ideal brewing conditions for green tea or french press coffee.

30. Energy-Monitoring Smart Outlets

Track appliance energy usage using app-connected outlets, helping you identify heavy energy draws to reduce electricity costs.

Upgrading Your High-Tech Kitchen

Smart kitchen technology has matured into practical, durable upgrades that simplify daily meal preparation and optimize food storage. By starting with one or two connected devices, such as an app-controlled coffee maker or smart refrigerator, you can experience high-tech convenience on any budget. Explore these smart appliance options, select features that match your lifestyle, and enjoy a modern cooking space!
