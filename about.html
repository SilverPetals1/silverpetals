Our Studio Story

Passion for Precious Metals & Natural Gems

Inspired by the artisan heritage of classic British craft institutions like Kernowcraft, Silver Petals was founded to bridge the gap between silversmithing knowledge and modern digital creators.

Our Three Studio Pillars

1. 100% Free & Accessible Jewelry Education

We believe high-level jewelry craftsmanship should not be locked behind expensive academies. From our 12-step wire wrapping guides to torch safety cheat sheets, every guide on Silver Petals is free to learn and share.

2. Ethical Sourcing & Recycled Eco-Silver

Every gram of sterling silver wire we supply is refined from certified recycled sources. Our natural gemstone cabochons — including our signature Flash Blue Labradorites and Rainbow Moonstones — are sourced directly from small family cutters who practice fair labor standards.

3. Designed for Pinterest Bloggers & Creators

We optimize all our articles for Pinterest SEO, providing high-resolution vertical 2:3 infographics, step-by-step supply checklists, and rich pins that empower creative entrepreneurs to build their own thriving audiences.

Get in Touch With Our Crafts Team

Have questions about wire gauges, gemstone compatibility, or collaborating on a Pinterest tutorial? Send us a message!
