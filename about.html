About SilverPetals™

Fine Botanical Jewelry & Radiant Gemstones

SilverPetals™ creates timeless, nature-inspired jewelry handcrafted from solid 925 sterling silver and hand-selected natural gemstones.

Our Brand Values

1. Certified Solid 925 Sterling Silver

Every piece is forged from pure, hypoallergenic solid 925 sterling silver, ensuring lifetime durability, nickel-free comfort, and a radiant polished shine.

2. Ethical Sourcing & Natural Gemstones

From natural rainbow moonstones to deep flash labradorites, our crystals and gemstones are responsibly mined and hand-selected for natural brilliance.

3. Nature-Inspired Botanical Design

Each ring, earring, and pendant is sculpted with organic petal motifs, floral textures, and balanced proportions designed for everyday luxury.
