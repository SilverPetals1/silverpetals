Feldspar Family

Labradorite (Spectrolite)

Known for its magical iridescence called labradorescence. Flashes in electric blue, green, and gold.

Mohs Hardness: 6.0 – 6.5
Heat Tolerance: Low (Do not heat torch)
Best Setting: Wire wrapping & rub-over bezel

Rainbow Moonstone

Exhibits a mesmerizing adularescence with blue sheen across milky-white stone. A top favorite for silver floral jewelry.

Mohs Hardness: 6.0 – 6.5
Heat Tolerance: Very Sensitive to thermal shock

Natural Turquoise

Classic blue-green copper mineral featuring dramatic matrix patterns. Porous and sensitive to chemicals and perfumes.

Mohs Hardness: 5.0 – 6.0
Heat Tolerance: Fragile! Discolors easily with heat
Best Setting: Serrated bezel strip & silver sheet

Mohs Hardness Scale & Silversmith Torch Guide

One of the most common beginner mistakes is heating a stone while soldering. Use this quick reference:

Gemstone Mohs Scale Daily Wear Ring? Torch Solder Safe? Recommended Setting
Diamond & Moissanite 9.25 – 10.0 ✔ Yes (Lifetime) ✔ Yes (High Heat) Prong, Bezel, Flush mount
Ruby & Sapphire (Corundum) 9.0 ✔ Excellent ✔ Yes (Borax coat) Any silversmith mount
Quartz, Amethyst, Citrine 7.0 ✔ Good ❌ No (Will discolor/crack) Set after all soldering is done
Labradorite & Moonstone 6.0 – 6.5 ⚠️ Moderate Care ❌ No (Thermal fracture) Wire wrapping or Bezel push
Opal, Turquoise, Pearls 2.5 – 6.0 ⚠️ Occasional Wear ❌ Strict NO (Burns/Ruins) Cold join, Glue, Bezel crimp
