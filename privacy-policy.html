Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. How SilverPetals respects your privacy, manages cookies, and ensures data protection.

1. Introduction

Welcome to SilverPetals (https://silverpetals.online/). We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you may share while browsing our jewelry editorial publication, guides, and curated collections.

2. Information We Collect

When you visit our site, we may collect minimal technical information to maintain site performance and optimize user experience:

  • Voluntary Communications: When you submit questions via our Contact form, your name, email address, and inquiry details.
  • Device & Analytics Data: Anonymous browsing metrics such as browser type, referring pages, pages visited, and time spent to improve article quality.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: Small text files to remember your preferences (such as checklist completions on guide tutorials).

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used strictly to:

  • Deliver relevant jewelry guides, bridal styling tips, and sizing calculators.
  • Respond directly to reader inquiries and customer support messages.
  • Prevent fraudulent activities and maintain website security.

4. Third-Party Links & Outbound Connections

Our articles may contain links to external third-party websites, social platforms (such as Pinterest), or merchant partners. SilverPetals is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of external websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

5. Cookie Policy

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to view and read our jewelry guides and editorial content.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Page.
