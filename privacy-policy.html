Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

SilverPetals.online collects standard, non-personally identifiable log information when you browse our home decor website, including browser type, referring pages, timestamps, and page visits. If you opt into our newsletter, we store your email address securely to send room styling guides.

When you click on an Amazon affiliate link on SilverPetals.online, Amazon places a tracking cookie in your browser to verify qualifying purchases under the Amazon Associates Program. These cookies adhere to Amazon’s privacy policy and standard affiliate tracking practices.
